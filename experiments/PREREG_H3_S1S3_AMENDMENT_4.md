**Commit SHA:** 8edf227

whose R0.2, R0.4, R0.6 and R7c this narrows, and

**Discovery record:** Deviation 13 in `DEVIATION_LOG.md`.

## What the registration does not cover

The registration defines each C1 construction on "landmarks present in the file,
matched by symbol through the pinned gene-info file". It treats presence as a
property of the gene axis. Two of the three released pseudobulk files carry
landmark columns that are present on the axis and undefined in the values, and
because R4's phenotype-positive arm reads one of those two files, three of the four
constructions are affected. The registration specifies no handling for an undefined
coordinate, so the implementation built directions on one, and `R0.4` read a median
that was not a number.

For the RPE1 release the cause is established: the released per-gem-group
z-normalization divides by a control standard deviation that is zero in GEM group
46 for ATF3 and group 9 for CCL2, measured on the raw single-cell file. For the
genome-wide release the six affected coordinates are recorded as deposited `+inf`
values and nothing more. Its single-cell file is 66 GB and the registration does
not estimate genome-wide reliability for that reason, so the control-variance
diagnostic that settled the RPE1 case has not been run on it, and no mechanism is
attributed to those six here.

## The rule

**C1. A construction's analysis basis.** For each construction built from an
externally sourced file, its analysis basis is the ordered intersection of the
frozen 978-landmark axis with the genes that source represents exactly once and
defines with finite values throughout. Coordinates failing that condition are
excluded only from constructions that read that source. No value is imputed,
clipped, substituted or reconstructed, and no observation is excluded on account of
one.

The condition is read off the source. It names no gene, it is evaluated before any
statistic is computed, and it cannot be satisfied or broken by a result, so a
coordinate's exclusion carries no information about the finding.

**C1a. The rows the condition is evaluated over.** The condition is evaluated over
every row of the source file, not over the rows a particular construction consumes.
R4's phenotype-positive arm is a row filter on the genome-wide file, so evaluating
the condition on its qualifying rows alone would let a phenotype decide which
coordinates exist. The basis is therefore a property of the file, and
C1-GW-phenotype-positive takes the same basis as C1-GW. This is stricter than
necessary: a coordinate undefined only in a non-qualifying row leaves the basis
anyway.

**C2. Exclusion is per source, not global.** A coordinate undefined in one release
stays in every construction whose source defines it. CCL2 leaves C1-RPE1 and the
drug vectors evaluated under C1-RPE1; it stays in C0, C1-K562, C1-GW and the shRNA
arm. The six coordinates the genome-wide file leaves undefined leave C1-GW and
C1-GW-phenotype-positive and stay elsewhere.

Comparisons between constructions are already registered on shared landmarks: R0.5
compares each pair "on shared landmarks" and R6a uses "landmarks present in both
files". A coordinate absent from one basis is therefore absent from any comparison
involving that construction and present in every other, with no further rule. No
target, drug, hairpin or pseudobulk row is removed because a coordinate was: a
target direction is built from the remaining coordinates whenever its rows are
otherwise defined and the result has a nonzero norm.

**C3. The bases, frozen.** `registry/frozen/analysis_bases.json`, generated by
`experiments/03i_freeze_analysis_bases.py` from the files on the volume the
analyses read, holds each basis as an ordered list of landmark gene ids with its
positions, its symbols, the per-coordinate counts of undefined values, and a
sha256 over the ordered gene ids.

Every basis is above the registered 500-landmark floor, and the floor is checked
against the basis rather than against the match.

**C3a. What the exclusions cost each registered comparison.** R0.5 and R6a compare
constructions on the landmarks their bases share, so the cost of an exclusion is
the change in those intersections and not the change in a single basis.

R0.5 compares C0 against C1, C1 against C1-RPE1 and C1 against C1-GW. R6a compares
C1 against C1-RPE1. Each intersection below is computed by `shared_space`, the same
helper the analyses use, and recorded in the bases artifact rather than entered by
hand.

| registered comparison | shared, as frozen | shared, with nothing excluded | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| R0.5: C0 against C1-K562 | 728 | 728 | 0 |
| R0.5: C1-K562 against C1-RPE1 | 688 | 688 | 0 |
| R0.5: C1-K562 against C1-GW | 712 | 718 | 6 |
| R6a: C1-K562 against C1-RPE1 | 688 | 688 | 0 |

C0 places its difference in the full 978 landmarks with zeros where the file
measures nothing, so its declared space is the whole axis and the intersection with
C1-K562 is C1-K562's basis. Its record names the two spaces apart,
`n_landmarks_declared` 978 and `n_landmarks_measured` 728, and carries the ordered
positions and a hash of each together with the scPerturb source's own sha256. The
728 coordinates C0 measures hash to `c5cc399e3e6c98c0`, which is C1-K562's basis
hash: the two coordinate sets are identical and not merely the same size, and a test
requires that identity.

CCL2 is not among the 728 landmarks the K562-essential file matches, so it was
never in the space R0.5 and R6a compare the two cell lines on. Excluding it changes
no registered comparison. It changes the within-arm C1-RPE1 quantities of R1 to R4
and R7, and R0.8's concentration under C1-RPE1, from 813 coordinates to 812. The
primary registered test of cell context, R6a, runs on the same 688 landmarks either
way.

The genome-wide exclusions cost six coordinates from the C1-against-C1-GW
comparison. The C1-GW-against-C1-RPE1 intersection, 675 against 680 unfiltered, is
not a registered R0.5 pair and is recorded for completeness.

C1-K562 covers 728 of the 978 landmarks, which is the primary construction's
coverage and a property of the release rather than of this rule. The registered
500-landmark condition is a usability floor; 728 coordinates are not evidence that
C1 represents the landmark space.

`geometry/references.py:validated_analysis_bases` is the one validator every
consumer calls, and it refuses unless all of the following hold: the bases file
hashes to the pinned value; the generator hashes to the pinned value; the schema
version is 1; the construction names are exactly the four above; each
record's source filename, source digest, basis digest, matched count and basis size
equal the pinned values; the landmark axis digest equals the pinned value and the
axis this run is using; each basis rehashes to its own recorded digest; each basis
is strictly ascending with no repeats; each basis's gene ids are this run's axis at
its own positions; each basis is above the floor; and C1-GW and
C1-GW-phenotype-positive agree on every field but their names. A construction is
additionally refused at load time unless the source file's digest equals the one
its basis record names, so a basis cannot be applied to a release other than the
one it was measured on.

`registry/frozen/expected_identities.json`, which Amendment 3 pins at sha256
`3de491d6234c9dc9f56072227b50e7a513fdc25720a9bc113ef56c0939e3b9b2`, is unchanged.
A frozen pin is not rewritten to carry a later amendment's values, so Amendment 4's
pins are a separate artifact.

**C3c. R0.5 computes all three registered pairs, with a signed cosine.** R0.5
registers "the cosine between each pair of constructions on shared landmarks: C0
and C1; C1 and C1-RPE1; C1 and C1-GW". The implementation computed the first two and
never built the third, so C1 against C1-GW was absent from a registered audit
module. It is added, on the 712 landmarks those two bases share, and the run refuses
unless R0.5's output holds exactly the three registered keys and each one ran on the
intersection the frozen bases record.

The cosine is signed. The implementation took an absolute value, and the
registration distinguishes the two deliberately: it defines `E = cos^2` as "signless
target-axis alignment" and `s = cos(m, u)` as "signed alignment", and asks R0.5 for
the cosine. An absolute value would report two constructions that assign a target
opposite directions as agreeing, which inverts what the module measures. R0.5 now
reports the signed per-target cosine and the number of negative ones.

Neither change is a change of criterion. R0.5 carries no reading that can upgrade a
manuscript claim, and it has not run.

**C4. R0.4, R0.6 and R7c are evaluated on the construction's basis.** R0.4
verification and R0.6 reliability for each C1 construction are computed on that
construction's frozen analysis basis, in one coordinate order for the single-cell
means and the released pseudobulk alike. R7c consumes only reliability estimates
defined on the same basis as the reference it restricts, and refuses where the
recorded basis hash differs from the reference's. Both modules previously ran on
each release's full gene axis, 8,749 genes for RPE1 and 8,563 for K562, which
verified one vector space and assessed another.

**C4a. The superseded run is not resumed into.** R0.4 and R0.6 ran once on each
release's full gene axis, and their shards exist. The amended run writes under a
directory keyed by the basis digest, so it starts empty, the diagnostic run that
exposed the defect stays where it is, and nothing is overwritten. Assembly refuses
any shard entry whose recorded basis digest is not the one being assembled, and
R0.4 and R0.6 refuse to start unless the preflight passed on the same bases file
this run validates. The R0-R7 driver refuses a single-cell audit whose recorded
basis digest is not the one it builds its own references on.

**C5. An undefined value after the freeze is refused, not excluded.** A non-finite
value on a coordinate the frozen basis holds stops the calculation. It is not
grounds for narrowing the basis a second time. The same refusal applies to a
non-finite cosine operand, a non-finite reliability, and a non-finite value in a
direction before normalization.

**C5a. Preflight.** Before R0.4 and R0.6 run,
`modal_03d_single_cell.py:stage_basis_preflight` confirms that each release lands
its frozen basis exactly once per symbol and that every value on those coordinates
is finite in both the single-cell and the pseudobulk file, and computes no
registered statistic. It writes and commits its report and then refuses, so a
non-finite operand stops the work with a record of what it refused rather than
being noted in a file nothing reads. `modal_h3_execute.py:stage_preflight_checked`
validates the bases artifact through the same validator and builds every C1
construction on it without computing a registered quantity.

**C6. R0.4 reports its counts, and refuses an undefined median.** R0.4 reports
`n_total`, `n_finite`, `n_non_finite` and a reason for every target with no cosine.
Where any per-target cosine is undefined, R0.4's status is `refused`: no median is
reported, and R0.6 and R7c take no label from it. The registered rule fires when
the median per-target cosine is below 0.99, and a median that is not a number is
not below 0.99; routing it into that branch labels a construction on arithmetic
rather than on disagreement. An undefined value is serialized as `null` beside the
target it belongs to, and a non-finite literal in an output file fails the write.

**C7. No further coordinate removal.** After this amendment is frozen, no
coordinate, target, drug, row or observation is removed from any analysis on
account of a quantity computed from it, and no basis is regenerated to change a
result. A basis changes only if a source file changes, in which case its sha256
changes with it and the run refuses until the basis is regenerated and recorded as
an amendment.

**C8. A corrected release, or the exact procedure.** The authors have been asked
for the normalization procedure, the definition of `core_scale_factor`, the
handling of a zero control standard deviation, and whether corrected files exist.
A corrected release or the exact code, if supplied, is analyzed as a separately
identified sensitivity analysis under its own identity, and does not replace a run
frozen under this amendment.

**C8a. A contract is not written as an assertion.** `python -O` removes `assert`
statements, so a refusal written as one is absent under that flag. Every refusal in
`geometry/` and `experiments/` raises instead, which the suite requires of every
file outside `experiments/superseded/`, where a script is a record of what ran and
is not edited. Running the suite under `-O` before this change failed five tests:
the collinear-covariate refusals in `geometry/inference.py` and
`experiments/03c_h3_sensitivity.py`, `load_bundle`'s ragged-input refusal,
`target_representatives`' one-direction-per-target refusal, and
`03_phenotype_projection.py`'s row-count refusal, which became an `IndexError`
instead. 147 assertions were converted mechanically and the conversion was checked
to preserve every message and every condition.

Two of the converted files, `03_phenotype_projection.py` and
`03b_h3_crispri_ground_truth.py`, produced numbers the deposited manuscript reports.
The rewrite changes no computation, and the versions those results were generated
under remain recoverable at their own commits, so hardening the executable copy does
not alter the deposited record. The hardening is committed on its own, before the
freeze, and computes nothing.

The suite passes 248 tests under both interpreters, and both logs are pinned
in C3b.

**C9. Freeze discipline.** The bases, the loaders, the refusals and the tests named
here are frozen with this file.

`tests/test_analysis_basis.py` builds releases whose undefined coordinate is a
different gene each time, so its tests pass by reading the source rather than by
special-casing a symbol. It requires that a coordinate leaves a basis only where
its source leaves it undefined; that the rule follows the source rather than a
named gene; that a gene outside the landmark axis never enters a basis whether or
not it is defined; that one source's exclusion leaves every other source holding
the coordinate; that a comparison between two bases is their intersection; that
every direction built on a basis is finite with unit norm; that an undefined
coordinate inside a frozen basis is refused rather than excluded a second time;
that a basis frozen on one file is refused on another; that a source lacking a
basis coordinate is refused; that an undefined median is refused rather than read
as below the threshold; and that no undefined value reaches a serialized output.

`tests/test_frozen_bases_identity.py` checks the artifact the registered run will
consume: that it passes its own external pin; that each construction holds the
counts and the excluded symbols stated in C3; that CCL2 leaves C1-RPE1 and that the
six genome-wide coordinates leave the two genome-wide arms and no others; that the
two genome-wide arms share one basis; that the registered comparisons run on the
intersections C3a states; that C0 declares the whole axis and measures 728; that
the axis file is the axis the bases were frozen against; that Amendment 3's pinned
identity file is unchanged; that an edit to the bases is refused even when it
recomputes the artifact's own digests; that a pin naming a different source digest
is refused; that a basis frozen against another axis is refused; that a changed
generator is refused; and that no analysis file outside `experiments/superseded/`
states a contract as a removable assertion; that R0.5 registers exactly the three
pairs and each runs on its frozen intersection; that R0.5 takes the signed cosine;
that C0 declares 978 and measures 728 with the two named apart; that the coordinates
C0 measures are C1-K562's basis; and that C0's source is pinned from outside the
artifact.

## Foreknowledge at the time of this amendment

R0.4 and R0.6 ran before this rule was written, on the full gene axis. What was
seen:

- For C1-RPE1, 2,393 targets of which 1,943 returned an undefined cosine. Over the
  450 that returned a finite one, the median was 1.0000000100052202, the minimum
  0.8097611585567198, and two fell below 0.99.
- For C1-K562, 2,057 targets, no undefined cosine, median 0.9999999986457878,
  minimum 0.03967811296898979, 116 below 0.99.
- That the RPE1 axis matches 813 of the 978 landmarks, that CCL2 is the only
  undefined coordinate among them, and that excluding it leaves 812.
- That the genome-wide file leaves six of its 721 matched landmarks undefined.
- That two reconstructions of the released normalization from the raw files fail at
  a maximum absolute difference of 1824.3968881650242, with the undefined positions
  agreeing exactly.

**R0.6.** The split-half reliabilities for C1-K562 and C1-RPE1 were computed in the
same run and written to `results/03d_h3_reference_discordance/single_cell_audit.json`,
sha256 `7e891153704cac61751f742bfcd27b5e11955148580403bb6c64ab5651b66292`. No R0.6 quantity has been examined: no reliability value, median,
distribution or per-target figure from that file has been read, reported, quoted in
any analysis artifact, or used to choose anything in this amendment. The file is
preserved at that hash as the record of the superseded run, and is not consumed by
R7c, which refuses an estimate whose recorded basis digest is not the one its
reference is built on.

**The genome-wide diagnostics.** What was seen before the rule was written is the
count and identity of the six undefined coordinates and their per-coordinate value
counts, produced by the generator itself. No R0.5, R4 or other genome-wide statistic
was computed or examined.

**C1-K562 in particular.** The finite filter does not narrow C1-K562: its basis is
its matched 728 landmarks, before and after. The repair nevertheless changes what
R0.4 and R0.6 compute for it, from the release's full 8,563-gene axis to that
registered 728-landmark basis, so the K562 full-axis median seen beforehand is
foreknowledge about a quantity that will change and is disclosed as such. It is not
reported, and the repaired computation is not described as numerically unchanged.

The 450-target median is not an estimate of the median on a frozen basis, and it is
not reported as R0.4. R0.4 and R0.6 are recomputed once under this amendment and
that recomputation is the result. The earlier numbers stay in Deviation 13 as a
record of what was in hand when the rule was chosen.

No R1-R7 statistic has been computed under any construction, and R0.5 has not run.

## Maximum claim under this registration

Confirmatorily, the paper may say: two of the three released Replogle pseudobulk
files leave matched LINCS landmark coordinates undefined, one coordinate in the RPE1
release and six in the genome-wide release, affecting three of the four
constructions built from them; in the RPE1 release the cause is a zero control
standard deviation in the released per-gem-group normalization, measured in the raw
single-cell file; the affected coordinates are named with their counts; under a
prospectively frozen rule those coordinates were excluded from the constructions
reading those sources alone, leaving every deposited finite value unchanged on bases
of 812 and 715 landmarks; and the R0-R7 readings computed on those bases are what
their registered criteria say they are.

It may not say that the genome-wide coordinates have the cause established for the
RPE1 ones, that the deposited RPE1 or genome-wide values are otherwise correct, that
the released normalization was reproduced, that excluding the coordinates leaves the
affected arms equivalent to unaffected ones, or that any R0.4 or R0.6 quantity
measured on a full gene axis means anything.

## What the paper may say afterwards

Nothing in this amendment changes a hypothesis, a criterion, an interval, a
permutation, a seed or a reading. It fixes which coordinates each construction is
defined on, and it fixes that an undefined value stops a calculation rather than
quietly entering one.
